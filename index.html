About Me

“Peonies, pit lanes, and paper.”

Hi! I'm Athena, and I go to Deerfield Academy. Most of my free time goes into drawing, writing, and animating — and when I'm not making something, I'm reading, watching a movie, or watching a race.

I'm also deep into fashion, JDM builds, and luxury cars. Ask me about brands, cars, F1, or books and I'll talk for hours.
