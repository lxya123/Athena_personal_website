About Me

“Peonies, pit lanes, and paper.”

Hi! I'm Athena, and I go to Deerfield Academy. Most of my time goes into making things — drawing, working on my novel, and animating to songs frame by frame. The rest goes into books, films, and shows; I read a bit of everything.

I'm into old money and high-end fashion, and JDM builds and luxury cars in equal measure. Race weekends belong to F1. Ask me about brands, cars, F1, or books and I'll talk for hours.
